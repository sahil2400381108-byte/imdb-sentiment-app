# Cinema artwork

The current frontend uses `cinema-neon.png`, an original asset generated with the built-in Imagegen tool. It depicts cinema props, not a real film or an IMDb poster. It is served locally without third-party requests.

## Final prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: 3:1 wide cinematic website hero background artwork, NOT a UI mockup. Create an original highly polished photorealistic 3D still life. All objects clustered on the RIGHT HALF: a tall classic red and white striped popcorn carton filled with golden popcorn, a black movie clapperboard tilted slightly, an antique dark metal cinema film reel and curling celluloid filmstrip. A few scattered popcorn kernels on glossy dark floor. Black cinematic studio backdrop with subtle deep indigo blue and purple rim lights, magenta glow reflected on the objects, amber light inside popcorn. Black clapperboard has NO lettering or writing, no text anywhere on carton or props. LEFT HALF almost entirely black/near-black negative space for an existing HTML headline. Rich tactile textures, realistic popcorn, premium dramatic studio lighting, crisp detailed props, professional film-review platform mood. Wide panoramic 3:1 composition, props fully inside right half with some negative margin. No UI, no buttons, no typography, no logos, no watermark, no people.

## Earlier concept

`cinema-hero.png` is a preserved earlier amber cityscape concept, not used by the final interface.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide cinematic hero backdrop for a sophisticated dark movie-review web application, not a UI mockup. Create a single ultra-wide 3:1 panoramic photographic film still. Scene: a lone small silhouette wearing a long coat standing on the RIGHT THIRD, looking into a vast brutalist science-fiction city at dusk, towering hazy monolithic architecture, desolate dark sand and fine atmospheric dust, a large pale amber sun partially obscured by buildings near upper right. Cinema 35mm film grain, breathtaking scale, rich charcoal blacks, restrained burnt amber highlights, desaturated bronze and sepia tones. Left third should be very dark with subtle architectural silhouettes, naturally empty negative space for white HTML heading overlay. Naturalistic cinematic cinematography, atmospheric depth, premium film festival visual. No words, lettering, logos, title, frames, panels or watermark. Not a real movie reference; original scene. Landscape wide 3:1.


## Typeface

Manrope is served locally as `manrope-variable-latin.woff2` (variable weight, Latin subset) from the Google Fonts distribution. License: `Manrope-OFL.txt` (SIL Open Font License). No font requests leave the app at runtime.
